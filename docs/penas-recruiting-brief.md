# Conectx Peñas Recruiting Brief

## Mission

Find and recruit the first 3 match-day test champions for Conectx among organized Liga MX peñas in CDMX.

The target is not broad awareness. The target is 3 real field tests with groups that can use Conectx in a live stadium environment where mobile data fails.

## Who We Are Looking For

We are not looking for generic fans, influencers, or the highest-follower accounts.

We are looking for the younger tech-forward lieutenant inside a peña. This is the person who already helps coordinate the group and can get 5 to 10 members to try a tool for one match.

### Best Signs of a Good Lieutenant

- posts match-day logistics
- shares meetup points or arrival details
- helps manage a WhatsApp group
- responds to group coordination questions
- handles bus, ticket, or entry organization
- is active on Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, or WhatsApp
- looks comfortable trying a new app

### Bad Targets

- fan accounts with no real-world group activity
- meme pages
- broad sports creators with no coordination role
- formal leaders who appear hard to access and unlikely to test personally
- accounts with large followings but no evidence of organizing real people

## Geography and Group Focus

Prioritize:

- CDMX peñas for América
- CDMX peñas for Pumas
- CDMX peñas for Cruz Azul
- active away-supporter groups that regularly travel into CDMX

Secondary targets:

- university supporter groups
- neighborhood fan clubs with regular stadium attendance
- travel coordinators attached to fan groups

## What Success Looks Like

A successful recruit is someone who:

- clearly helps coordinate a real group
- can get 5 to 10 people into one Conectx squad for one match
- is willing to try a new tool for one live test
- can give fast feedback after the match
- can send a short video or voice note if the test works

## What We Are Pitching

Do not pitch Conectx as a social network, startup partnership, or general-purpose chat app.

Pitch it as a lightweight coordination tool for when stadium signal dies.

The core promise:

**Set one shared offline stadium plan before the match, so the group still knows where to regroup when WhatsApp fails.**

## Offer

The ask is small:

- one 10-minute intro
- one match-day test
- one lieutenant champion
- 5 to 10 members in the squad

Do not ask for a big commitment, public endorsement, or broad rollout up front.

## Qualification Rule

When choosing between two leads, always prefer:

**clear evidence of coordination responsibility over audience size**

One person who already runs logistics is more valuable than a much bigger account with passive followers.

## Research Workflow

For each candidate peña:

1. Confirm the group appears real and active.
2. Identify the likely lieutenant, not just the main page.
3. Capture proof they help coordinate real people.
4. Find the best contact path.
5. Score the lead.
6. Add a short note on why this person could pull in 5 to 10 users.

## Lead Data to Capture

For every lead, capture:

- peña name
- team
- city
- home stadium
- platform
- profile URL
- contact name or handle
- likely role
- evidence of logistics responsibility
- estimated ability to recruit 5 to 10 users
- contact path
- status
- notes

## Lead Scoring Template

Score each category from 1 to 5.

### 1. Coordination Signal

- 5: clearly runs logistics or group coordination
- 3: some evidence of helping organize
- 1: fan account only, no coordination evidence

### 2. Group Reality

- 5: clear evidence of a real recurring peña with in-person attendance
- 3: likely real but lightly evidenced
- 1: unclear or mostly online fandom

### 3. Access Probability

- 5: direct DM path, likely responsive
- 3: reachable but indirect
- 1: hard to contact or likely ignored

### 4. Pilot Potential

- 5: can likely bring 5 to 10 users to one match test quickly
- 3: possible but uncertain
- 1: unlikely to activate a real test

### 5. Tech Openness

- 5: clearly experiments with tools or digital coordination
- 3: neutral
- 1: likely resistant to trying anything new

### Total Score

Add all five categories. Prioritize highest total score, with a tiebreaker in favor of stronger Coordination Signal.

## Outreach Rules

- Keep it short.
- Be concrete.
- Do not use startup language.
- Do not oversell mesh or technical architecture.
- Do not ask for “partnerships.”
- Ask for one small live test.
- Mention the specific problem: signal dies, WhatsApp fails, people get split.

## Outreach Script, Instagram / Facebook DM

Hola, vi que ustedes sí coordinan gente real para los partidos. Estamos probando una app pensada para cuando se cae la señal en el estadio y el grupo se separa. La idea es que tengan un plan offline de punto de reunión y reagrupación que siga funcionando aunque WhatsApp falle. Buscamos probarla en un partido con una persona que ya ayude a coordinar y 5–10 miembros del grupo. ¿Te interesaría verla 10 minutos esta semana?

## Outreach Script, Warm Intro

Te escribo porque estamos probando una app para grupos que van juntos al estadio. Sirve para dejar cargado un plan offline de punto de reunión y reglas de reagrupación, para que el grupo no dependa de que WhatsApp siga funcionando cuando se cae la señal. Buscamos una primera prueba con una peña y alguien que ya ayude a coordinar al grupo. ¿Crees que tenga sentido presentárnosla a la persona que mueve esa parte?

## Outreach Script, Follow-Up

Te lo resumo fácil: no estamos buscando una campaña ni nada grande. Solo queremos probar si un grupo de 5–10 personas puede reagruparse más rápido al salir del estadio cuando no hay datos. Si te hace sentido, te enseño cómo funciona en 10 minutos.

The recruiting team should produce:

1. A list of 30 to 50 candidate peñas in CDMX.
2. A ranked top 10 lieutenant target list.
3. Contact paths for each top target.
4. Evidence notes showing why each target is a real coordinator.
5. Platform-specific outreach copy.
6. A live tracker of replies and booked intros.

## Final Reminder

This is not a brand-awareness project yet.

The only thing that matters right now is getting to 3 real match-day tests with the right kind of operator inside the right kind of group.
