## Strategic Context

### The Barras Unidas Opportunity

In March 2026, multiple barras signed an unprecedented peace accord called "Barras Unidas de Mexico" ahead of the World Cup. Signatories include Ritual del Kaoz (America), Sangre Azul (Cruz Azul), Barra 51 (Atlas), Sobredosis (Necaxa), La Komun (Santos), La Curva Norte (Pumas), La Tito Tepito (Atlante), and Barra de Puebla.

**Why this matters for Conectx:** This accord signals:
1. Barras are actively thinking about World Cup readiness
2. There is cross-group communication infrastructure we can tap into
3. The messaging is positive ("passion, not fear") -- Conectx fits this narrative
4. A single introduction to a Barras Unidas contact could cascade to 8+ groups

### Liga MX Away Fan Ban

Liga MX has imposed restrictions on organized away fans ("visitantes") at stadiums. This has NOT stopped groups from traveling -- it has pushed coordination underground, making digital tools MORE important, not less. Groups like La Adiccion and Barra Insurgencia have been sanctioned specifically for defying these bans.

**Why this matters:** These groups already have sophisticated unofficial coordination channels (WhatsApp, Telegram). Conectx's mesh networking adds a layer that works when those channels fail (inside packed stadiums with no cell service).

### World Cup Fan Fest Infrastructure

CDMX will host the FIFA Fan Fest at the Zocalo (capacity 60,000-100,000 daily). The Estadio Azteca hosts the opening match June 11 and 5 total games. This creates a massive density of organized fan groups in confined spaces -- the exact scenario where cell service collapses and Conectx proves its value.
