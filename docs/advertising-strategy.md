# Conectx Advertising Strategy

## Core Advantage

When cell networks are congested at stadiums, Conectx is the **only app working**. That's premium, captive attention — no competing notifications, no doomscrolling. Hyper-local targeting (stadium + match + city) without any personal data or GPS tracking.

## Ad Formats

### Sponsored Match Cards
- Brand logo/name on match cards in the schedule screen
- e.g., "Partido presentado por Corona"
- Non-intrusive — data is already there, logo sits alongside

### Meetup Point Sponsorship
- Named meetup landmarks instead of generic pins
- e.g., "Meet at the Tecate Zone — Gate 4"
- Useful to fans, not annoying

### In-Chat Native Cards
- Subtle cards between messages (never interrupting conversation)
- e.g., "Pide tu Uber Eats al estadio" or local vendor promos
- Frequency-capped, only during pre-match or halftime lulls

### Branded Reactions / Stickers
- Sponsored emoji reactions (e.g., a Coca-Cola celebration, Adidas goal)
- Fun for users, high visibility for brands

### Pre-Match Push Offers
- Deal notifications before match starts
- e.g., "Tu squad tiene 6 personas — pide una cubeta con descuento"
- Group-oriented promos that fit the squad context naturally

## What to Avoid

- **No banner ads** — kills premium feel and user trust
- **No user data selling** — contradicts the privacy promise
- **No ads during active mesh mode** — P2P bandwidth is precious, don't waste bytes
- **No video ads** — wrong context, people are at a live match
- **No personal data targeting** — only contextual (stadium, match, city)

## Revenue Models

| Model | Effort | Value to Advertisers |
|-------|--------|---------------------|
| Sponsored schedule | Low | Medium |
| Meetup sponsorship | Low | High |
| In-chat cards | Medium | High |
| Push offers | Low | Medium |
| Branded reactions | Low | High |

## The Pitch

> "100,000 fans in a stadium with no cell service, and we're the only app that works. Your brand is the one they see."

Target advertisers: World Cup sponsors (Coca-Cola, Adidas, Visa), Mexican brands (Tecate, Telcel, Oxxo), food delivery (Uber Eats, Rappi, Didi Food).

## Implementation Priority

1. **Branded reactions** — smallest surface area, high perceived value, zero user friction
2. **Sponsored meetup points** — layers onto existing meetup feature
3. **Sponsored match cards** — layers onto existing schedule screen
4. **In-chat native cards** — requires ad serving logic, frequency capping
